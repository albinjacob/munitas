Roles reference

What each role can and cannot do. 11 roles.

How to read this page

The same everywhere. The roles and what they may do are the same in every organisation. An organisation chooses who holds each role, and a person can hold more than one.

What a role cannot do. Where a role cannot do something, that is usually on purpose: the person who asks for something is never the only check on it, and whoever approves an act cannot afterwards give independent assurance over it.

Reading data. Every role held by a person reads only published data without asking. More sensitive data is requested through a lease, which a person who is not the asker approves.

Roles held by people

The staff of an organisation that uses Munitas.

Data custodian

Answers for the data a department owns, and decides who may read it.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Approves or refuses a request to read data that their department owns, for a person or for an agent.
  • Approves or refuses a request for a role, for people in their organisation.
  • Opens the files of a version when the platform finds a reason to: a development sample drawn for them, synthetic files, or files under a lease. Every opening is logged.
  • Confirms another person's claim about how sensitive a dataset is.
  • Adds or removes the approvers of their department, and names cover while one is away.
  • Brings data in.
  • Confirms that a role given to somebody is still needed.
  • Closes down an organisation, with a reason that is recorded: a custodian closes their own organisation, and a platform administrator can close any.
  • Sees the storage housekeeping record of their own organisation.
  • As an approver of a department, gives the department's part of an agent change, suspends an agent that the department owns, and assigns its sponsor.

Cannot

  • Cannot decide whether a de-identified result may be released.
  • Cannot register code.

Network architect

Decides which outside hosts an agent version may call.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Approves or refuses the outside hosts that an agent version asks to call.

Cannot

  • Cannot decide a version that they uploaded themselves.
  • Cannot approve a request to read data.
  • Cannot register code.

Data engineer

Brings data in, registers code and runs the jobs. Does not read what the jobs produce.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Brings data in.
  • Registers code: agents, agent versions, pipelines and pipeline versions.
  • Starts pipeline runs.
  • Opens the files of a version when the platform finds a reason to: a development sample drawn for them, synthetic files, or files under a lease. Every opening is logged.
  • Asks for a lease to see real values of locked data, for a stated purpose and a short time.
  • Asks for a development sample of real recordings, to build and test a pipeline version.
  • Reports an incident about an agent.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data.
  • Cannot decide whether a de-identified result may be released.

De-identification reviewer

Judges whether removing identifying details worked well enough for a result to be released.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Decides whether a de-identified result may be released, and chooses the de-identification standard for it.
  • Opens the files of a version when the platform finds a reason to: a development sample drawn for them, synthetic files, or files under a lease. Every opening is logged.
  • Asks for a lease to see real values of locked data, for a stated purpose and a short time.
  • Asks for a development sample of real recordings, to build and test a pipeline version.
  • Reports an incident about an agent.
  • May review what an agent produced, unless the same person started the run.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data.
  • Cannot register code.

Privacy auditor

Checks how locked data was used, from evidence that holds no names and no values.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Reads the evidence of how locked data was used: who read, who was refused, and which keys were opened. It never holds a name or a value.
  • Asks for a lease to see real values of locked data, for a stated purpose and a short time.
  • Makes the evidence pack of an agent, and checks that its records have not been changed.
  • Reports an incident about an agent.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data, so that the checking stays independent.
  • Cannot decide whether a de-identified result may be released.
  • Cannot register code.

Data protection officer

Watches how the organisation handles personal data, advises on it, and may stop an agent.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Sets the de-identification standards: how much identifying detail a released result may still hold.
  • Suspends an agent, which stops new runs at once.
  • Makes the evidence pack of an agent, and checks that its records have not been changed.
  • Reads the evidence of how locked data was used: who read, who was refused, and which keys were opened. It never holds a name or a value.
  • Asks for a lease to see real values of locked data, for a stated purpose and a short time.
  • Reports an incident about an agent.
  • Confirms that a role given to somebody is still needed.
  • Sees the storage housekeeping record of their own organisation.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data, so that oversight stays independent of what it oversees.
  • Cannot register code.
  • Cannot decide whether a de-identified result may be released.
  • Cannot write the risk levels, confirm the level of an agent or give the second approval on a change to an agent, because those decide how agents are held.

AI risk owner

Answers for how the organisation holds its AI agents.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Writes the approved tools list: the tools a new version of an agent may add without a request, each with the highest risk level it is approved for.
  • Writes the risk levels that say how strictly agents are held.
  • Confirms the risk level of an agent.
  • Gives the officer's part of an agent change, which is the second approval at the High level. Resumes an agent that needs it, and may assign a sponsor and re-confirm an agent.
  • Suspends an agent, which stops new runs at once.
  • Makes the evidence pack of an agent, and checks that its records have not been changed.
  • Reports an incident about an agent.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data.
  • Cannot register code.
  • Cannot decide whether a de-identified result may be released.

Roles held by platform staff

The people who run and support the platform itself. They hold no more standing access to an organisation's data than any other role.

Support and reliability

Keeps the platform running and supported, with telemetry and never records.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Sees the storage housekeeping record of every organisation, which names no data.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data.
  • Cannot reclaim storage or close down an organisation.

Platform administrator

Runs the platform itself. Holds no standing access to what is inside an organisation.

Reads without asking
Published data only. Anything more sensitive needs a lease that somebody else approves.

Can

  • Suspends an agent, which stops new runs at once.
  • Makes the evidence pack of an agent, and checks that its records have not been changed.
  • Reports an incident about an agent.
  • Reclaims storage, and must give a reason that is recorded beside what was removed.
  • Records or approves a legal hold, never alone: one administrator records the notice and a different one approves it.
  • Closes down an organisation, with a reason that is recorded: a custodian closes their own organisation, and a platform administrator can close any.
  • Sees the storage housekeeping record of every organisation, which names no data.

Cannot

  • Cannot approve a request to read data.
  • Cannot register code.
  • Cannot decide whether a de-identified result may be released.
