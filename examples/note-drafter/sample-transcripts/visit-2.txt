Visit 2, cardiology outpatient clinic. A made-up conversation, for demonstration only.
Doctor: Hello, please sit down. How can I help?
Patient: My heart has been racing at night, on and off since last month.
Doctor: Do you drink coffee in the evening?
Patient: Two cups most days. I am allergic to penicillin, and my mother had heart trouble too.
Doctor: I am going to listen to your heart now. Your heart rate is 104 and your blood pressure is 128 over 80.
Doctor: The rhythm is a little fast but regular.
Doctor: I think this is likely to be caffeine and poor sleep, though I am concerned enough to check for an irregular rhythm.
Doctor: I will refer you for a heart monitor worn for 24 hours, and advise you to stop coffee after noon.
Doctor: Book a follow up in three weeks.
Patient: That sounds fine.
