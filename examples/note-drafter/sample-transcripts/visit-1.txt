Visit 1, cardiology outpatient clinic. A made-up conversation, for demonstration only.
Doctor: Good morning. What has brought you in today?
Patient: I have had tightness in my chest when I climb stairs, for about two weeks.
Doctor: Does it spread anywhere, or ease when you rest?
Patient: It goes into my left arm, and it eases after a few minutes of rest.
Patient: I take a tablet for blood pressure every morning, and I stopped smoking last year.
Doctor: Let me examine you. Your blood pressure is 150 over 92 and your pulse is 88.
Doctor: I can hear a regular rhythm and no murmur.
Doctor: This sounds like angina brought on by effort, and I suspect narrowed arteries.
Doctor: I will order an ECG and an exercise stress test, and start a low dose of aspirin.
Doctor: Please avoid heavy exertion until the test, and come back in two weeks to go through the results.
Patient: Thank you, doctor.
